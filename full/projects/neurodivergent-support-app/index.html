Neurodivergent Support App (In Progress)

A guided companion with configurable routines, sensory check-ins, and low-stimulus UI patterns.

Tech: TypeScript, React, Node.js, PostgreSQL

Highlights

  • Co-designing with students to define calming color palettes and typography that avoid sensory overload.
  • Building routine templates (class, study, commute) with reminders and adjustable pacing.
  • Planning a privacy-first analytics layer to surface gentle nudges without tracking PII.

System Architecture

  • React front-end with offline-friendly caching; minimal motion and strong focus states.
  • API layer in Node.js with PostgreSQL for templates and user preferences; feature-flagged rollout.
  • Background jobs to batch reminder deliveries and feedback summaries without spamming users.

Ownership & Learnings

Leading design and build; currently prototyping flows, defining accessibility guardrails, and stubbing the API.

This project is co-designed with neurodivergent classmates to make daily routines feel calmer and predictable. The UI defaults to low-stimulus colors, strong focus indicators, and motion reduced by default. Routines come with adjustable pacing and optional reminders.

I'm prototyping the React front-end with offline caching, while the Node/PostgreSQL backend will store templates and preferences. Analytics are opt-in and anonymized; the goal is to provide gentle, user-controlled insights rather than surveillance.
