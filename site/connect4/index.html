How the AI decides

The board is stored as a bitboard — the whole 6×7 grid packs into a pair of integers, so checking for four in a row is a handful of shifts and masks instead of a scan. That makes the search fast enough to look a long way ahead.

On top of that sits negamax with alpha-beta pruning: the engine explores its move, your best reply, its best answer to that, and so on, abandoning any line that already proves worse than one it has found. Positions it has already evaluated go into a transposition table, so the same position reached by a different move order costs nothing the second time. Iterative deepening searches one ply at a time until the clock runs out, keeping the best answer it completed.
