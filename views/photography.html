Behind the Lens

Photography is an exercise in seeing the world differently. This collection represents a diverse array of disciplines—from patience-testing landscape photography and split-second street candids, to meticulous macro detail work.

Shooting in uncompressed RAW format allows for maximum dynamic range, letting me pull details out of the deepest shadows of a coastal cliff, and the brightest highlights of an overcast sky.

Disciplines Showcased

  • Landscape: Coastal views and forests
  • Street: Urban candids and culture
  • Macro: Vibrant close-up flora
  • Atmospheric: Mist, fog, and weather
