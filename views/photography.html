Through the Lens

Newfoundland Road Series

A collection of atmospheric captures from a misty drive through Newfoundland's boreal wilderness. Shot on a Nikon DSLR, these images explore the interplay between fog, forest, and the open road — finding beauty in the quiet, in-between moments of a journey.

About This Series

This series was captured during a drive through Newfoundland's interior — a landscape defined by its boreal forest, Atlantic fog, and endless stretches of road. The images embrace the moody, overcast atmosphere rather than fighting it, finding a quiet drama in the interplay of mist, spruce, and asphalt.

All photographs were shot handheld from the passenger seat, capturing the raw, unfiltered perspective of the journey as it unfolded.

Gear & Settings

  • Camera: Nikon DSLR
  • Format: RAW (NEF) + JPEG
  • Style: Atmospheric / Documentary
  • Location: Newfoundland, Canada
